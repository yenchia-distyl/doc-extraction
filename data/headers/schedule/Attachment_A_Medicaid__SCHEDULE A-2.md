# Attachment A: Medicaid

Attachment A: Medicaid
SCHEDULE A-2
GOVERNMENTAL CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS
This Schedule A-2 sets forth certain provisions specific to the Oklahoma SoonerSelect Children's Specialty
Program ("Specialty Program").
1.
Provisions Incorporated from Schedule A-1. Except as may be expressly provided in this Schedule
A-2, all definitions and requirements set forth in Schedule A-1 shall apply with respect to Participating Providers
participating in the Specialty Program network, and are hereby incorporated into this Schedule A-2, except that for
purposes of this Schedule A-2, all references to (i) the "State Contract" and all State Contract citations set forth in
Schedule A-1 relating to the Medicaid and CHIP Program shall be understood to refer to the same numbered
provisions of the State Contract relating to the Specialty Program; and (ii) the "SoonerSelect Program" shall be
understood to refer to the Specialty Program.
2.
Staff Training. Upon request of Company, Provider shall make available its staff for training on the
requirements, policies, and procedures of the Specialty Plan. (State Contract $1.4.6.8)
3.
Coordination with Other State Agencies. Provider shall participate in stakeholder meetings as
requested by OHCA, DHS or OJA. (State Contract 1.4.6.10)
4.
Covered Services. Provider shall familiarize itself with all Covered Benefits provided under the
State Contract and, where applicable to Provider, shall comply therewith. (State Contract $1.7.1) If Provider provides
behavioral health Covered Services, Provider shall familiarize itself with Sections 1.7.1.2 - 1.7.1.6 of the State
Contract and where applicable to Provider, shall comply therewith. (State Contract $1.7.1) Providers of pharmacy
services shall familiarize itself with all of Section 1.7.2 of the State Contract and, where applicable to Provider, comply
therewith. (State Contract $1.7.2)
5.
Behavioral Health Crisis Services. Providers of Behavioral Health Crisis Services shall cooperate
and comply with Health Plan's policies and procedures to ensure: (i) mobile behavioral health crisis services provided
to children in the care and custody of DHS or OJA do not duplicate crisis services provided through another service
(e.g., TFC); and (ii) DHS and OJA are notified of all behavioral health crisis services provided through any modality
(face-to-face, telehealth, or the hotline) to children in the care and custody of the respective agency, within one
business day in a manner established between the Health Plan and OHCA. (State Contract $1.7.1.6)
6.
Early and Periodic, Screening, Diagnostic, and Treatment (EPSDT). Providers of "Early and
Periodic, Screening, Diagnostic, and Treatment (EPSDT)," shall provide screening and assessment services that align
with the standards specified by the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and Child Welfare League of America
(CWLA) for children and teens in foster care. (State Contract, $1.7.11)
7.
Pharmacy Program. Provider shall apprise itself of the Oklahoma Pediatric Psychotropic Medication
Guidelines ("Guidelines") and any updated guidelines released by the State and Provider shall participate in any
education and training required by Company relative thereto. Provider will cooperate with Company's monitoring of
utilization of psychotropic medications and Company's reporting to the OHCA thereon. (State Contract $1.7.2.1)
8.
Provider Payment. The Company shall reimburse the following services as follows:
8.1
Targeted Case Management (TCM) services shall be reimbursed at the current Medicaid
rate;
8.2
RBMS shall be reimbursed at the current Medicaid rate; and

8.3
TFC, ITFC, and Multi Systemic Therapy shall not be reimbursed at a rate lower than the
current Medicaid rate.
(State Contract $1.16.1.11)
9.
Medical Management & Medically Necessary Services. Medically Necessary Covered Services
provided in the Specialty Plan must be furnished in a manner that:
a.
Is no more restrictive than that used in the Oklahoma Medicaid program, including
quantitative and NQTLs, as indicated in State statutes and regulations, the Oklahoma Medicaid State Plan, and other
State policies and procedures;
b.
Addresses the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of a Covered Person's disease,
condition, and/or disorder that results in health impairments and/or disability;
c.
Allows Covered Persons to achieve age-appropriate growth and development;
d.
Allows Covered Persons the ability to attain, maintain or regain functional capacity; and
e.
Considers the role of non-medical factors (e.g., placement, placement changes, involvement
with the CW and/or the JJ system). (State Contract $1.8)
Services are considered Medically Necessary if they meet the requirements in Appendix 1B: "Definitions" of this
State Contract, OAC 317:30-3-1(f), and federal requirements described in 42 C.F.R. § 438.210(a)(5). For example,
OAC 317:30-5-741 (c) specifies Medical Necessity criteria for TFC and OAC 317:30-5-751 (c) specifies Medical
Necessity criteria for intensive treatment family care. Company will include service specific Medical Necessity
criteria set forth in OAC as a component of the applicable PA process unless alternative criteria is proposed by
Company and approved in writing by OHCA and Provider shall be bound thereby. (State Contract §$1.8.1 and 1.8.2)
10.
Open Access Prior Authorization Process. Company may establish policies and procedures for an
open access prior authorization approach for certain Specialty Plan Covered Persons, certain Covered Services, and
certain Provider types. In such event, Provider shall apprise itself of those policies and procedures and will be bound
thereby. (State Contract 1.8.6)
11.
Education and Training of the OHCA, DHS, OJA, Judicial system, and Stakeholders. Provider shall
participate in required training developed by Company in coordination with OHCA, DHS, and/or OJA relative to the
processes and procedures of accessing services through the Specialty Plan. (State Contract $1.8.7)
12.
Care Management and Population Health. Provider shall cooperate and comply with Company's
care management model and policies and procedures for case management and intensive case management for
children, youth, and young adults with special health care needs including those with chronic conditions and who are
medically-complex. (State Contract $1.9)
13.
Screening and Assessment Tools. Provider shall cooperate with Company's incorporation of
screening tools and/or assessments (e.g., Child and Adolescent Needs and Strengths (CANS)) utilized and/or
recommended by the state into Company's care management processes as directed by the OHCA. (State Contract
$1.9.2)
14.
Provider Network Development and Contracting Standards. Company shall not contract with any
Provider that OHCA, OHS or OJA has terminated or refused to recontract. (State Contract $1.13.1.1.2)
